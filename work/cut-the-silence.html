How it works

Three steps. One command.

Most business owners record themselves talking, then dread sitting down to clean it up. That part is now ours.

01

You send your recording

Drop your raw recording into our shared folder. Any length. Audio or video.

02

The AI does the editing

It listens through the whole thing, marks every silent gap, every breath, every dead-air moment. Then it cuts them out and stitches your voice and video back together.

03

You get back the cleaned version

Your voice and rhythm stay. The dead air is gone. Tight, ready to post.
