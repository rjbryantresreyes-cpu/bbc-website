Home Vision Studio

Home Vision Studio is a design and materials showroom in Cedar Park, Texas, serving homeowners, interior designers, and builders. Balay ni Bruno & Co. partners with them on their online store and the systems behind it, turning a large catalog of tile, wood, and cabinets into a guided, confident way to choose.

Visit homevisionstudio.com ↗ Cedar Park, TX Shopify e-commerce + showroom Homeowners · Designers · Builders

Home Vision Studio is one of the most technically involved partnerships we run. They carry hundreds of materials and serve three different kinds of buyers at once. Our job is to make their online store feel less like a warehouse and more like a showroom, where every visitor finds their way to a confident decision and a real conversation.

Below are the systems we have built together. Each one started as a real problem for the showroom and became a repeatable way of working. We have written most of them up as plain-English guides so any business owner can see how the approach works.

The systems we built

Design Center Curated Material Walls

Hundreds of products turned into a handful of curated walls by design personality. Six tile walls (A to F) and five wood walls (A to E), so shoppers choose by feeling instead of fighting filters.

See the approach → Cabinet Design Center Good, Better, Best Cabinet Tiers

Custom cabinets sold in three clear tiers (Value, Semi-Custom, Custom) so buyers place themselves by budget, avoid sticker shock, and arrive at a consultation already warm.

See the approach → Product System Placement Labels That Keep It Tidy

A tagging system that drops each new product into its exact spot automatically, with protected fields so the curated layout never breaks as the catalog grows.

See the approach → Conversion Mood Board Ordering

Materials do not fit a normal cart, so shoppers pin what they love to a mood board and send it to the showroom, plus a simple way to book a visit. No cold checkout.

See the approach → Website Guided Showroom Redesign

The plan to turn a flat catalog into a guided design experience, with curated navigation, the mood board, and a booked visit, all built as a safe copy first.

See the approach → Growth Builder & Designer Referrals

A preferred-partner program and outreach system that gets local builders and interior designers to bring their own clients through the showroom doors.

See the approach → Process Reliable Preview Links

How we share work for review on a link that is always online and phone-ready, completely separate from the live store, so nothing in progress ever touches production.

Ongoing support

Alongside the build, we support the partnership across the day to day:

  • Content for three audiences. Copy and posts written separately for homeowners, designers, and builders, so each one hears a message meant for them.
  • Email marketing. Building and warming a list of builders and designers, with sequences that keep the showroom front of mind.
  • Social media. Ongoing content across Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, and LinkedIn.
  • Search visibility. Helping the right local buyers and pros find the showroom online.
  • On-brand product copy. Descriptions written to match each curated group, so the whole store sounds like one confident brand.

Why this partnership matters

  • Home Vision Studio proves Balay ni Bruno & Co. can build custom online store systems, not just themes: curated discovery, tiered pricing, and a mood board that replaces the cart.
  • Every system started as a real showroom problem and became a repeatable way of working we can bring to any business with a big, considered catalog.
  • We build carefully and protect the live store: concepts are reviewed as private previews before anything goes live.
