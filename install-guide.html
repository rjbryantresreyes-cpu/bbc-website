The Road Map

Seven Steps to a Working System.

You do not need to be a developer. You just follow each step in order. A Balay ni Bruno & Co. team member walks you through the whole install on a screen share call.

01
Foundations

Install the Four Free Tools

Google Drive for Desktop, Node.js, Git for Windows, and Claude Code. Free, official downloads from each company. We install them together over screen share.

Drive for Desktop Node.js Git for Windows Claude Code
02
Open Your AI

Open Your AI Family Folder in Claude Code

Your AI Family lives in our shared Drive, already accessible from your computer. We open it in Claude Code and run a first prompt to confirm your AI speaks in your brand voice.

Google Shared Drive
03
Brand Rules

Install Your Always Loaded Brand File

We deliver a custom rules file built from your onboarding form. You save it to one folder on your computer. From that moment forward, every AI session loads your brand voice automatically.

Custom CLAUDE.md
04
Connectors

Connect Your Business Tools

Authorize each tool you use. Required: Drive, Gmail, Calendar. Optional: Slack, Asana, GitHub, Canva, HeyGen, and others. After this step your AI can read and act in the apps you already use.

Drive Gmail Calendar Slack Asana GitHub Canva HeyGen
05
Memory

Activate Persistent Memory

Your AI reads every file in your Family folder and builds a memory of who you are, who is on your team, your customers, and your top priorities. From now on, it remembers across every conversation.

User memory Project memory Brand voice rules
06
Skills

Install Your Custom Skills

We pre build a few skills your AI can run with a single command. Type a slash, pick the skill, get a full workflow. Examples: weekly priorities review, inbox triage, social caption batch.

/weekly-priorities /inbox-triage /social-caption
07
First Real Task

Run Something Real, End to End

This is the moment your AI proves it. Pick a real task that matches your role. Watch it read your files, hit your tools, and produce something useful. From here, you are working.

Founder prompt Ops prompt Marketing prompt Technical prompt
Once You Are Live

This Is What Changes.

Four real examples of what your AI can do once the install is complete. Pick the one closest to your role.

For Founders & CEOs

Your Monday Morning Brief

"Read my last 10 emails, the next 7 days of my calendar, and my open priorities. Tell me what I should focus on tomorrow."

Result: A clear focus list, drafted in your voice, ready before your coffee is.

For Operations

Client Check In, At Scale

"Look at every client folder in my Drive. Tell me which clients have not had an update in 14 days, and draft a check in email for each."

Result: A batch of personal emails ready to review and send, in minutes instead of hours.

For Marketing

A Week of Content, Drafted

"Read my brand voice rules and the last 5 social posts I published. Draft 5 new captions in the same style for next week."

Result: Five on brand captions ready to schedule, all matching your existing voice.

For Technical Founders

Your Weekly Ship Recap

"Read my GitHub recent commits and tell me what shipped this week. Draft an update email I can send to the team."

Result: A clean weekly update email, written from your actual commit history.

Before Install Day

What You Need to Have Ready.

A short prep checklist. We will walk through this on a quick call before your install day, but you can confirm everything below in about 15 minutes on your own.

If anything on the list is missing, we wait to schedule the install until it is sorted. A clean prep means a smooth install.

Pre Install Checklist
  • ✓A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer with admin rights
  • ✓A Claude Pro or Claude Max subscription, active
  • ✓Your work Google account ready to sign in
  • ✓Access to the shared Drive folder we sent you
  • ✓A list of business tools you want connected
  • ✓An install slot blocked on your calendar with our team member
