Can My Online Store Have a Light and Dark Mode Customers Can Switch?

Yes. We add a small sun and moon switch to your store. A shopper taps it and the whole site flips between a light look and a dark look, and your store remembers the look they chose as they browse. Same store, same products, two comfortable moods.

Some shoppers love a bright, airy look. Others find a dark, low light look easier on the eyes, especially at night. For a long time, a store owner had to pick one and hope it suited everyone. You do not have to choose anymore. You can offer both and let each shopper decide for themselves.

The way it works is simple. We put a small switch in your store, usually a sun and a moon up in the header. Tap the moon and the store goes into its dark look. Tap the sun and it comes back to the light look. It is the same store underneath, with the same products and the same layout, just dressed in two different color moods.

What the shopper sees

Here are the two moods side by side. Same products, same pages, just a different feel. The shopper picks whichever one they prefer.

☀ Light look
Bright and airy

Soft, warm tones. Clean and open. The familiar daytime feel most shoppers expect.

☽ Night look
Calm and easy

Deep, restful tones. Easier on the eyes in low light, with the same gold accents.

It remembers their choice

This is the part that makes it feel polished instead of gimmicky. Once a shopper picks light or dark, your store keeps that choice. As they move from page to page, it stays in the look they chose. When they come back later, the store opens in their preferred mood right away. They never have to set it twice.

The switch is not just a toggle. Your store remembers each shopper's choice, so the experience feels personal and stays consistent every time they visit.

A recent example

We recently rebuilt the store of a hair brand we work with into a refined, editorial look. As part of that, we added a light and night switch right in the header. The shopper can tap between a bright daytime look and a calm low light look, and the store holds onto whichever one they choose.

We built the whole new design quietly off to the side first, so their real store kept selling the entire time. The owner reviewed it on a private preview link from her phone before anything changed. When she gives the final go, it becomes live in one step, with the old version kept as an instant backup.
