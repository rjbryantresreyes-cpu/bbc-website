Can Someone Run My Google Ads for Me?

Yes. Google Ads management is now part of our in-house toolkit at Balay ni Bruno & Co.: the keyword research, the ad copy, the landing pages, the tracking, and the ongoing management, built directly inside an account you own.

Most small businesses that try Google Ads on their own end up with the same result: money spent, few conversions, and no clear read on why. The platform rewards constant tending, keyword lists that get pruned, ad copy that gets tested, budgets that get watched, and that is exactly the kind of ongoing work that is easy to set up once and then never touch again.

We added Google Ads management to what we run for clients so that "set it up right and keep tending it" is not left to chance.

What "running your Google Ads" actually means

Not just turning a campaign on. A real Google Ads setup has several moving parts that all have to work together, or the budget leaks.

1
Research

Keyword research for what your customers actually search, plus a negative keyword list so budget does not get spent on searches that were never going to convert.

2
Build

Ad copy written for your business, and a landing page built to match what the ad actually promises.

3
Track

Conversion tracking wired up before launch, so every dollar spent can be tied back to a real result, not a guess.

4
Manage

Once it is live, someone is actually watching it: adjusting bids, pausing what is not working, and reporting back in plain language.

Why it is built inside an account you own

We work directly inside your own Google Ads account rather than routing the work through an account tied to us or a third party. It takes a little longer to set up correctly, but it means the campaign history, the account structure, and the data belong to you from day one, no matter what happens to the relationship later.

The account you advertise from should be the account you own. If a campaign only exists inside someone else's login, you do not actually have it, you are borrowing access to it. We set it up the other way around.

Why we built this in-house

Paid search is one of the few channels where the results are visible almost immediately, which also means the cost of a sloppy setup is visible almost immediately. Rather than refer that work out to a separate vendor, we built the capability to research, build, launch, and manage a Google Ads account ourselves, using the same direct account access and reporting we already use for the rest of a client's marketing.

That means Google Ads is not a bolt-on handed to a stranger. It sits next to the SEO, content, and website work we already do, so the strategy behind all of it stays connected instead of living in three different vendors' heads.
