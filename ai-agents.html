THE WORKFLOW

How They Work Together

The human leads. The agent supports. Together, they move faster without losing the personal touch.

1

Bruno or the team sets the direction

Every agent task starts with a human decision. What do we need? Who does it serve? The team defines this. Always.

2

The right agent is assigned

Based on the task type, the matching agent takes it on. Research goes to Cleo. Content goes to Nova. Operations goes to Rex.

3

The agent builds the draft or output

The agent works fast and brings back a complete first version. Not perfect. Ready for the human to shape into something great.

4

The human reviews and refines

The team member who partnered with the agent adds their judgment, voice, and care. That is what the client actually receives.

5

Delivery happens under the human's name

The client always works with a real person. The agent stays behind the scenes. The relationship stays human.

6

The loop improves every time

Each cycle teaches the system. The agents get better at the studio's style. The humans get more time to focus on what matters.

THE PRINCIPLES

The House Rules

Every family has rules. These three govern how the AI side of the house operates.

🌙

Rule 1: They Never Sleep

The agents work whenever the team needs them. 2am brief. Sunday research sprint. Midnight proposal draft. They are available without asking, without overtime, without burning out.

🧠

Rule 2: They Learn from Bruno

Every agent is guided by the same values Bruno built into this studio. The warmth. The directness. The care for quality. They do not have opinions. They carry the studio's standards.

🤝

Rule 3: They Work FOR People, Not Instead of Them

No agent makes a client decision. No agent replaces a conversation. Every output goes through a human before it leaves the house. That is non-negotiable, now and always.
