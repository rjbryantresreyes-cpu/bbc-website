Your Trust Matters

Privacy Policy

How we handle your information, in plain language. No fine-print games, no selling your data.

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Balay ni Bruno & Co. ("we," "us," or "our") runs this website and works with business owners as their operational support studio. This policy explains what we collect when you visit balaynibruno.co or reach out to us, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us at rj@balaynibruno.co and a real person will answer.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill out a form on our site, book a call, or email us, we collect what you choose to share. That usually means your name, email address, business name, and the message you send. We only ask for what we need to have a useful conversation with you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use analytics to understand how visitors use our pages. This includes things like which pages you view, how you arrived, your general location (city or region, not your exact address), and basic device and browser details. We use Google Analytics for this, and it relies on cookies. None of it identifies you personally to us.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your message and continue the conversation you started
  • To understand your needs so we can recommend how we might help
  • To improve our website and the content we publish
  • To send you follow-ups or updates, only if you asked for them or reached out to us first

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

The tools and services we use

We rely on a few trusted services to run the site and respond to you. Each handles your data under its own privacy terms:

  • Netlify hosts our website and processes form submissions.
  • Google Analytics helps us see how the site is used.
  • Calendly handles call bookings when you schedule time with us.
  • Email and messaging tools we use to reply to you and stay in touch.

We share information with these providers only as needed to operate, and only the information required for them to do their job.

Cookies

Cookies are small files saved on your device. We use them mainly for analytics, to understand site usage. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings at any time. The site will still work, we just see less about how it is used.

How long we keep your information

We keep what you send us only as long as we need it to help you or to meet a legal or business requirement. If you would like us to delete your information, just ask and we will.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. To do any of these, email rj@balaynibruno.co and we will take care of it.

Children

Our site and services are meant for business owners and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page. For meaningful changes, we will make the update clear.
