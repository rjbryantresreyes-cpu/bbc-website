About Ryan

Background & Expertise

Ryan Crismin Bernaldez is a 3-year digital marketing specialist who lives at the intersection of creative content and intelligent automation. His core focus is building AI-powered content pipelines — combining ChatGPT for scripting, HeyGen for avatar-driven video, and ElevenLabs for voiceover production — to create high-quality, scalable content for businesses in the home services and local market space.

Beyond AI workflows, Ryan is a full-stack content operator: he manages social media accounts end-to-end, produces short-form video and graphic design through CapCut and Canva, writes SEO-aligned content, and handles lead generation and CRM management for the clients he supports. His strength is the ability to own the entire content lifecycle — from strategy and scripting to production, scheduling, and publishing.

At BBC, Ryan supports clients in the home services industry with social media management, AI content production, and digital marketing tasks that require both creative skill and systematic execution.

Approach & Strategy

How Ryan Works

The strategic frameworks, workflows, and content philosophies that guide how Ryan delivers results for clients.

01

AI Content Workflow System

Combines ChatGPT (scripting) → HeyGen (avatar video) → ElevenLabs (voiceover) → CapCut (editing) into a single production pipeline. One workflow produces multiple content formats simultaneously — short-form video, voiceover ads, and script-based posts.

02

Content Batching

Rather than creating content piece by piece, Ryan batches production sessions — scripting, designing, and editing in volume. One session produces enough content for weeks, keeping publishing consistent without daily production time.

03

Platform-Optimized Content

Every piece of content is adapted for the platform it lives on — format, aspect ratio, caption length, hook style, and call-to-action are adjusted to maximize engagement on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

04

AI-Assisted Scripting

Uses ChatGPT to generate, test, and refine scripts before production. Scripts are tailored to the client's voice, target audience, and the specific platform — ensuring content feels natural, not robotic, while being produced at AI speed.

05

Visual Brand Consistency

Builds Canva template libraries for each client so all graphic design stays on-brand — same color palette, fonts, and style across all social content, eliminating the need to redesign from scratch each time.

06

Workflow-First Operations

Approaches every client relationship with a system mindset — tasks are organized, deadlines are tracked, and deliverables follow a repeatable process. Communication is proactive, updates are regular, and nothing falls through the cracks.

AI & Automation

AI Toolkit & Workflows

Ryan's AI stack isn't a collection of disconnected tools — it's a connected workflow where each tool plays a specific role in a content production pipeline.

🧠

ChatGPT — Scripting & Copy

Used for video script writing, caption copy, blog posts, and content ideation. Ryan uses ChatGPT to generate platform-specific scripts that match the client's voice and audience.

🎭

HeyGen — AI Avatar Video

Creates AI-generated spokesperson videos from scripts — branded avatars that deliver professional video content without filming. Used for promotional content, service explainers, and social media video.

🎙️

ElevenLabs — AI Voiceover

Generates professional-quality AI voiceovers from scripts. Used for video narration, ad voiceovers, and content that requires a consistent audio brand presence.

✂️

CapCut — Video Editing

Video editing for short-form content — assembles AI-generated video clips with graphics, music, captions, and transitions for platform-ready output.

🎨

Canva — Design & Graphics

Used for all graphic design work — social media graphics, branded templates, promotional materials, and visual content that supports the written and video content strategy.

📊

Meta Business Suite

Content scheduling, publishing management, and basic analytics for Facebook and Instagram. Used to maintain consistent publishing cadence without manual daily posting.
