About Vi

E-Commerce Built Right, Social That Actually Converts

Vi builds and manages e-commerce brands from the inside out. She handles Shopify website builds, product page creation, AI-assisted imagery, and email marketing flows, then ties it all together with social media content that keeps the audience engaged and the brand growing.

Her workflow blends platform expertise with AI-powered tools, using Midjourney for product imagery, Claude for copy strategy, and Yotpo for email and loyalty systems. The result is a digital brand that feels fully staffed, even when it's just her behind it.

At BBC, Vi currently leads the full digital support for S. Riviere Hair Collection, managing their Shopify build, product taxonomy, social content, email flows, and SEO. She handles social media and content for the AJ Battle Foundation across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook. She also built and managed the website for Exquisite AV, grew ESE Socials across platforms, and supports SEO blog content and website updates for Blackbelt Commerce.

Working Approach

How Vi Works

01

Storefront First, Social Second

The website is the foundation. Vi builds the Shopify infrastructure first, so when social drives traffic, the destination is ready to convert.

02

AI-Augmented Production

AI tools aren't a shortcut, they're a multiplier. Midjourney for imagery, Claude for copy, Yotpo for automation. The quality stays high; the output scales.

03

Platform-Native Content

Instagram content looks and feels like Instagram. TikTok content moves like TikTok. Vi adapts format, tone, and rhythm to where the audience actually lives.

04

Email as a Retention Engine

Every purchase is an opportunity to build a long-term customer. Email flows, especially through Yotpo, are built to bring people back, not just say thank you.

05

SEO Built Into the Product

Product page copy is written with search in mind from the start, so organic traffic builds naturally as the catalog grows.

06

Brand Consistency at Scale

Every post, email, and page reflects the same brand identity, same tone, same visual standards, same promise. Consistency is what makes a brand feel real.
