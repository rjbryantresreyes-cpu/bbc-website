Insurance & Cost

Care should never be blocked by paperwork

We keep this simple and clear up front, so there are no surprises.

Self-pay & transparent pricing

We work outside the usual insurance maze so your care is guided by what you need, not by what a plan will approve. Our team walks you through cost before you commit, in plain numbers.

Injury cases & Letters of Protection

Were you hurt in an accident? We work with attorneys through Letters of Protection, so you can get the brain injury care you need now and settle the cost as part of your case. We coordinate directly with your legal team.
