Who They Are

Real Clinical Care, Across Six Texas Cities

Elevate Clinics is a brain injury and mental health group serving patients across Texas. The team works in psychiatry, neurology, neuropsychology, ketamine therapy, and recovery from concussion and traumatic brain injury, with telehealth and in person care.

The care is strong. What holds it back is everything around the care: phone heavy intake, thin location pages, almost no patient education content, and social accounts that do not yet match the size of the practice. That gap is where new patients get lost. It is also the easiest part to fix.

Where We See Room to Grow

Five Opportunities, Ranked by Impact

We looked at the website, the search results, the social accounts, and the way patients reach you today. These are the five moves that would bring the most new patients for the least friction, in order. There is also a sixth, built for the attorney referred side of the practice.

1
High Impact

Patient Intake & Scheduling, Made Simple

Right now a patient has to download a form, print it, fill it, and the practice has to chase the rest by phone across six locations. We replace that with a clean digital intake the patient finishes on their phone, that flows straight to your team, plus online booking and automatic appointment reminders. Less phone time, fewer no shows, and far fewer patients who give up before they ever book.

The result: the people who already want your care actually make it to the calendar, instead of getting lost in the paperwork.

2
High Impact

A Content Engine That Answers Real Questions

People search before they call. They type things like concussion recovery, what is ketamine therapy, or signs of a brain injury. Today none of those searches lead to you. We build a steady stream of plain language articles and short videos that answer the exact questions your patients ask, so Elevate shows up at the moment someone is looking for help. Around eight to twelve pieces over the first three months, then a reliable rhythm after that.

The result: every clinic becomes easier to find on Google, and your expertise does the selling before a single call.

Mai · Content & Marketing Sonny · Social Your BBC VA
3
Strong Value

A Real Page for Each of the Six Locations

A six city practice needs six strong local pages, each one built to rank for that city and to show the clinicians who work there. We create a dedicated page per location with the right local search terms, clinician bios, services offered there, directions, and the behind the scenes signals Google uses to rank local results. This is one of the fastest ways to win patients who search by city.

The result: someone searching brain injury clinic in Frisco or psychiatrist in Austin finds your clinic, not a competitor.

Webby · Website & Local SEO Your BBC VA
4
Strong Value

Social Accounts That Match the Practice

The accounts exist, but the posting is light and scattered across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. We bring them onto one calendar, build on the Elevate Essentials video idea you already started, and add short videos for YouTube. Warm, clear, trustworthy posts that show the people and the care, not stock photos. Everything is drafted for you and nothing goes out without your approval.

The result: when a patient or a referring doctor checks you out online, the practice looks as established as it actually is.

Sonny · Social Your BBC VA
5
Strong Value

A Patient Story & Clinician Video Library

Nothing builds trust in healthcare like a real story and a face. We build a library of short videos: anonymized patient recovery stories told with full privacy care, friendly clinician introductions, and simple explainers for treatments like ketamine therapy and concussion recovery. These work everywhere, on the website, on social, and in the reminder emails a nervous new patient reads before their first visit.

The result: a hesitant patient sees someone who got better and a doctor who feels human, and books with confidence.

6
Add On

The Attorney Referred Side, Handled Cleanly

For the brain injury cases that come through law firms, the work is mostly coordination: tracking referrals, keeping records and letters of protection organized, and staying in steady contact with each firm's case managers so nothing stalls. We build a simple tracking system for these cases and a calm outreach rhythm to your referral firms, so the relationship stays warm and the paperwork never falls behind. We have already gathered a starting list of thirty one Texas personal injury firms that work this model, ready to nurture when you give the word.

The result: a steadier flow of attorney referred cases, with the admin load carried by the partnership instead of your clinical staff.
