About BBC

Built Around Trust, Collaboration, and Organized Execution

Balay ni Bruno & Co. is a systems-oriented support agency built around a close-knit team of trusted collaborators, family members, and skilled assistants helping businesses manage digital operations with more structure and clarity.

Unlike traditional VA agencies, BBC stays actively involved during planning, setup, workflow organization, and early execution — so clients receive guidance, support, and long-term operational direction, not just task execution.

The Process

How Our Managed Support Works

A structured process built around your business — not a generic onboarding flow.

1

Book a Call

We learn about your business, workload, goals, and the kind of support you need. This is a real conversation — not a form.

2

Match the Right Assistant

The founder personally selects a team member whose skills, working style, and experience align with your specific needs.

3

Plan the Setup Together

The founder, client, and assistant work together to organize tasks, tools, workflows, priorities, and the first operational roadmap.

4

Build the System

We set up workflows, marketing tasks, content systems, websites, email processes, automation structures, or operational frameworks.

5

Ongoing Support + Strategy

After setup, your assistant handles ongoing work. The founder stays involved for strategy sessions and quarterly planning. You are not left alone.

BBC stays actively involved throughout — setup, execution, and long-term strategy.
