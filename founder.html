The Founder Balay ni Bruno & Co.

Meet RJ, the Founder of Balay ni Bruno & Co.

He started with one client and a skill he taught himself at night. More than five years later, he has worked across real estate, e-commerce, social media, and lead generation, and now he builds the AI systems that run a whole studio. This is the background behind the work.

RJ Bryan Tres Reyes, who his team also calls Bruno, is the founder and lead strategist of Balay ni Bruno & Co. He is based in the Philippines and has spent years working U.S. and international hours for brands that needed someone who could both think and build.

His path did not start with a title. It started with real work for real people, one gig at a time, and a habit of learning the next skill on the job instead of waiting until he felt ready.

From a graphics gig to a whole craft

RJ began as a freelance graphic designer. While he was making social posts and brand graphics, he taught himself video editing at night and put it to work on real client projects. Graphics led to video. Video led to content systems. Content systems led to operations, marketing, and eventually building AI tools that do the heavy lifting.

That pattern, learn by building something real for someone who needs it, is still how the whole studio works today.

How he works

RJ runs the studio the way he started it: relationship first. Early on, an agency told him to drop his first client or lose his final paycheck. He kept the client and let the paycheck go, because she was a person who took a chance on him, not a transaction. That choice is still the operating principle of the whole company.

He would rather tell you the honest answer than the comfortable one. If there is a better way to do something, even when it costs a little more, he will say so. If a plan is weak, he will tell you. That is what a real partner does.

He is a systems thinker who stays hands-on. He likes to build the thing, prove it works, then hand over a system the client actually owns. The goal is never to make a business depend on him. It is to make a business stronger and freer.
