Special Projects

These came out of knowing a client's business well enough to see a problem they had stopped noticing. None of them were on the original scope.

An AI helper for every client, trained on their own business

Wooden Woodworks · S. Riviere · Freccia · BILT 2 Travel · Cherry Sage · AJ Battle · JR Signing · Home Vision Studio

Woody, Olivia, Bob, BILL, Ivy and the rest. Each one is a real AI helper trained on that client's brand, products, voice and rules, so it answers like someone who works there. The client owns it. It does not sound like a chatbot because it is not built like one.

A 3D cabinet configurator

Wooden Woodworks

Custom cabinetry is hard to sell from photographs because every piece is different. So we built a 3D configurator that lets a customer see the piece before it exists. Same idea now extends to walkable 3D room tours.

A billing system that reconciles itself

JR Signing Services

Matching signings to invoices to payments was eating hours every week. We built the reconciliation across QuickBooks, Google Sheets, Gmail and GoHighLevel, including a nightly safety check that catches updates a simpler trigger would miss.

Onboarding and agreement signing that runs without anyone touching it

JR Signing Services

When a prospect reaches the right pipeline stage, the welcome packet and service agreement go out on their own and come back signed. Built natively inside GoHighLevel so there is no extra subscription to carry.

Daily website health reports, sent automatically

Home Vision Studio · S. Riviere Hair Collection

Every morning the site gets checked, one fault gets fixed, and the client gets a branded report showing exactly what happened. They never have to ask how the website is doing.

A live availability light

Cherry Sage

Her whole business depends on whether she is available right now. So the site shows it: green when she is online, orange when she has stepped away, red when she is done for the day. A small thing that removes a question every visitor was silently asking.

An instant quote calculator

JR Signing Services

Prospects wanted pricing before they would book. Now they get it on the website, immediately, instead of waiting on an email that might come tomorrow.

Lead engines built per client, not bought off a shelf

JR Signing Services · S. Riviere · Wooden Woodworks · Freccia

A verified lead database for notary work, a stylist partnership pipeline for hair, builder and remodeler lists for cabinetry, and commercial outreach for construction. Each one researched for that specific business rather than pulled from a generic list.

Search and analytics reporting that pulls itself

First Class Finishes · Hi-Up Roofing

Weekly Google Search Console and GA4 numbers gathered automatically, so reporting is real data rather than screenshots taken by hand.
