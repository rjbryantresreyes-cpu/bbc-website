Can My AI Read and Reply to My Business WhatsApp Messages?

Yes. We connected an AI helper to WhatsApp so it can read your past chats, search across every conversation, and write a reply for you to approve before it sends. It never sends on its own. Here is how we set it up and what it does for you.

Most business owners run a real chunk of their business inside WhatsApp. Quotes, questions, follow-ups, the customer who asked about a job last week. It all lives in there. The trouble is, WhatsApp was built for chatting, not for keeping track. A message you needed slips down the screen, and by the time you scroll back to it, you have forgotten three other ones.

So we did something simple. We connected an AI helper to WhatsApp. Now it reads the past chats, searches across every conversation, and can pull up the full history of any chat in seconds. When a customer messages you, it writes a reply for you, ready to go, and you decide whether to send it. The AI does the reading and the drafting. You stay in control of the sending.

How the Connection Works

You do not install anything strange or hand over a password. It links to your existing WhatsApp number with a one-time QR scan. It is the exact same "Link a Device" screen you already use when you open WhatsApp on a computer. You scan it once, and that is the whole setup. After that, the AI keeps reading new chats automatically as they come in.

1
Scan once

A one-time QR scan on the same Link a Device screen you already use for WhatsApp on a computer. You do it once.

2
The AI captures every chat

Every conversation gets saved automatically, so nothing a customer told you gets lost in the scroll. Each chat can sit in its own folder.

3
Daily summary and ready-to-send replies

A short daily summary of what each customer said, plus a drafted reply for any chat that needs one. You read it and tap send.

Before and After

The change is not about doing more. It is about nothing slipping through. Here is what the day looks like before the AI is connected, next to after.

Before

  • Messages get lost in the scroll
  • You forget who you still owe a reply
  • You search by hand to find an old quote
  • No clear picture of what each customer said

After

  • Every conversation is saved and searchable
  • A short daily summary of what each customer said
  • The full history of any chat in seconds
  • A drafted reply waiting for your approval

Once it is set up, the part of your day that used to be "scroll, search, try to remember" becomes "read the summary, approve the replies." Same inbox, far less digging.

ⓘ

The safety rule: the AI drafts, you send. It never sends a message on its own. Every reply waits for you to read it and tap send. That is our drafts-first rule, and it is there so you are always the one who decides what your customers actually hear from you.

We Tested It on Our Own WhatsApp First

We did not try this out on a client. We connected our own Balay ni Bruno & Co. WhatsApp first. On the first connect, it pulled in thousands of past messages and dozens of contacts in a couple of minutes, then kept capturing new ones as they arrived. That is how we know it works before we ever bring it near your account.

And to be very clear: we never expose a client's private WhatsApp data. The proof on this page is our own demo, not anyone else's messages. Your conversations stay yours. The AI works inside your account to help you, and nothing leaves it without you.

The AI reads and drafts. You approve and send. Nothing reaches your customers until you say so, and nothing about your chats is ever shared outside your account.
