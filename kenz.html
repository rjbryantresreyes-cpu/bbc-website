About Kenz

Background & Expertise

Kenz Lloyd D. Villaflores is a Social Media Manager and AI Operations Support Specialist with three years of experience across real estate, e-commerce, and digital startup environments. He's managed social media for brands with 100K+ followers and supported content operations that have contributed to companies with 3M+ YouTube views, bringing the same level of organization and commitment to every client he works with.

Kenz's work lives at the intersection of content and operations. He builds and maintains content calendar systems in Google Sheets, runs targeted lead generation through Apollo and Instant Data Scraper, manages CRM pipelines, and handles the operational workflows that keep business backends clean and accountable. He's comfortable inside Shopify for e-commerce operations, and equally at home managing social channels, writing AI-assisted captions, and tracking deliverables across multiple clients.

At BBC, Kenz supports clients who need someone to own their social presence and operational layer, a reliable hands-on partner who keeps things moving, tracks what matters, and brings systematic thinking to every task he takes on.

Approach & Strategy

How Kenz Works

The strategic frameworks, operational systems, and content philosophies behind Kenz's work.

01

Content Calendar System

Kenz builds content calendar systems in Google Sheets that plan weeks of content in advance, mapping posts by platform, date, format, and goal. This keeps publishing consistent and removes the reactive "what do we post today?" problem.

02

Lead Generation Pipeline

Uses Apollo for targeted prospect research and Instant Data Scraper for volume list building, then organizes leads into structured Google Sheets pipelines with status, source, and follow-up tracking built in from day one.

03

Google Sheets Operational Tracking

Custom tracking systems built in Google Sheets for every client engagement, deliverable logs, content schedules, lead databases, and operational task lists. Everything documented, nothing assumed, all accountable.

04

AI-Assisted Caption Writing

Uses ChatGPT to draft, refine, and batch social media captions that match each brand's voice and audience. Copy is always reviewed and adapted, AI accelerates output, human judgment controls quality.

05

Platform-Specific Social Strategy

Adapts content format, tone, posting cadence, and call-to-action to each platform's specific audience behavior, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube require different approaches, and Kenz applies each one deliberately.

06

Organized Operational Mindset

Every task gets tracked. Every deliverable gets documented. Kenz approaches client work like an operations manager, systematically, with clear processes, consistent follow-through, and full visibility into what's done and what's next.
