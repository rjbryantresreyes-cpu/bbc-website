About Al

Design, Development, and the Detail That Makes It All Hold Together

Al builds and manages the digital and creative layer for the clients she supports, Shopify websites, Wix builds, landing pages, email marketing, graphic design, and social media, all handled with the same eye for detail and brand consistency.

She works across the full creative and technical spectrum. On the development side: Shopify website builds, product organization, theme management, and Wix site maintenance. On the creative side: graphic design, email marketing, social media content, and brand visual consistency.

At BBC, Al serves as BBC Handler for Home Vision Studio, managing their Shopify website, design center, social media, and email marketing. She also handles design and operational support for the AJ Battle Foundation, keeping both the nonprofit and real estate brand ecosystems visually consistent and operationally supported.

Working Approach

How Al Works

01

Website as Foundation

Everything points back to the website. Al builds Shopify and Wix sites that are ready to receive traffic from social, email, and campaigns, not just beautiful, but functional.

02

Design That Scales

Every graphic, template, and visual system is built to be reused and extended, not one-off pieces that take forever to replicate. Consistency at scale requires systems, not just talent.

03

Brand Cohesion Across Channels

The Shopify site, the social posts, the email templates, and the landing pages all look and feel like the same brand. Al maintains the visual thread across every touchpoint.

04

Email Built for Relationships

Email isn't just broadcast, it's conversation. Al writes and designs emails that feel personal and valuable, not like mass marketing. That's what keeps open rates healthy.

05

Product Organization as UX

How products are organized affects how customers shop. Al approaches product taxonomy as a user experience problem, clear categories, smart naming, and intuitive navigation.

06

Multi-Client Discipline

Working across multiple client ecosystems requires strict organization. Al keeps each brand's assets, guidelines, and workflows clearly separated and consistently maintained.
