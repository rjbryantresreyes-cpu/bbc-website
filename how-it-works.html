Stage 01

The Strategy Call

Everything starts with a real conversation. The founder — RJ — gets on a call with you to learn about your business, understand your direction, and identify exactly what kind of support you need. This is not a sales pitch. It is a working session where we figure out together what needs to happen.

What happens here:

  • — RJ asks key questions about your business, goals, and current situation
  • — You share what's working, what isn't, and what you need help with
  • — RJ provides initial strategies, suggestions, and recommendations based on what he hears
  • — We discuss the skill sets BBC can bring to your business
  • — We determine if BBC is the right fit and discuss next steps
01 Discovery
Stage 02

Contract & Welcome

Once we are aligned and you are ready to move forward, we formalize the partnership. You sign the contract, and from that moment, you are officially part of the BBC family. RJ personally introduces you to the assistant who will be working with you — and the team is formed.

What happens here:

  • — Contract is signed and the engagement is confirmed
  • — You receive a warm welcome email from BBC
  • — RJ personally matches and introduces your dedicated assistant
  • — You are connected to your support team
  • — The working relationship officially begins
02 Contract & Welcome
Stage 03

Onboarding Questionnaire

Before we build anything, we need to understand everything. You receive a detailed onboarding questionnaire that covers your business in depth — your brand, your audience, your tools, your goals, and your current operations. The more we know, the better we can build.

What happens here:

  • — You complete a thorough onboarding questionnaire at your own pace
  • — We gather all account access, tools, brand assets, and business information
  • — RJ reviews every answer personally
  • — Any gaps or questions are clarified before planning begins
  • — This becomes the foundation for your entire strategy
03 Onboarding
Stage 04

Planning & Strategy

With the questionnaire complete, RJ builds your full operational strategy. What to build first. What marketing to run. What systems to set up. What the priorities are. What the roadmap looks like. Nothing gets built until the plan is clear and agreed upon.

What happens here:

  • — RJ develops a customized strategy based on your questionnaire answers
  • — We identify what needs to be built, organized, or improved
  • — Marketing approach, content plan, and operational priorities are mapped out
  • — A clear roadmap with timelines is created
  • — Strategy is presented and finalized with the client
04 Planning
Stage 05

Build & Setup

This is where everything gets built. Your assistant — guided by RJ — sets up your website, marketing systems, content workflows, automation, CRM, operations structure, and everything else your business needs. We build it properly from the start so it works the way it should.

What happens here:

  • — Website, landing pages, or digital presence built or reorganized
  • — Marketing workflows and content systems set up
  • — Automation, CRM, and operational tools configured
  • — Schedules, time frames, and content calendars established
  • — Everything tested and reviewed before moving to ongoing support
05 Build & Setup
Stage 06

Ongoing Support & Strategy

With everything in place, your assistant takes on the day-to-day workload. But BBC does not stop there. RJ stays actively involved — reviewing performance, providing updated strategies, and making sure your business keeps moving forward. This is a long-term partnership, not a handoff.

What happens here:

  • — Your assistant handles daily and weekly tasks and execution
  • — RJ stays in the loop on all major decisions and directions
  • — Monthly strategy calls available to review, plan, and adjust
  • — Analytics reviewed regularly to identify what is working and what needs to improve
  • — New campaigns, projects, and initiatives planned continuously
  • — Beginning of each year: full business planning session with RJ
06 Ongoing Support
