The Process

Five Steps. One Guiding Principle.

We've designed every stage to feel collaborative, not complicated. Here's exactly what working with BBC looks like, from the very first conversation.

01

Discovery Call

We start with a real conversation. Bruno personally joins every discovery call to understand where you are, where you want to go, and what kind of support would actually make a difference. No scripts, no sales pitch, just an honest conversation about your business and what you actually need.

Personal · No Pressure
02

Understanding Your World

Before we build anything, we learn. Your tools, your team, your goals, your frustrations. We take time to understand the full picture, not just the surface ask, but the context behind it. This is where most providers skip ahead. We don't. We'd rather take an extra week to understand your business than spend three months building the wrong thing.

Deep Listening · Full Context
03

The Right Match

Based on your needs, Bruno matches you with the right team member or members. Not whoever is available, the right fit for your business and your personality. We believe the quality of a working relationship directly affects the quality of the work, so we take this step seriously and never rush it.

Intentional Matching · Not Random
04

Systems & Setup

We set up the workflows, documentation, and communication systems that keep everything organized and nothing slipping through the cracks. From onboarding documents to recurring task flows, we build a clear structure that works for how you actually operate, not how a template says you should.

Organized · Nothing Lost
05

Active Ongoing Support

We show up consistently. Regular check-ins, clear updates, and a team that's genuinely invested in your success, not just completing tasks. We grow with you, adapt as your needs change, and remain a steady presence in your business for the long term. This is where most of the real work, and the real relationship, happens.

Consistent · Long-Term Thinking

Practical Expectations

What Working With Us Actually Looks Like

We believe transparency builds trust. So here's a clear picture of what to expect when you work with BBC, the day-to-day reality of our collaboration, not just the highlights.

You'll always know who's working on your account, what they're working on, and how to reach them. No mystery, no guesswork, no silence.

⏱️
Response Times
Same-day responses for standard requests during agreed working hours. Urgent items are flagged and handled accordingly. You'll never be left wondering what's happening.
💬
Communication Style
Clear, direct, and warm. We communicate in plain language, no jargon, no fluff. Updates are proactive, not reactive. You won't have to chase us down for information.

Regular Check-Ins
We schedule regular syncs based on your preferred cadence, weekly, biweekly, or monthly. These aren't status updates, they're real working conversations about what's next and what's changed.

Handoffs & Documentation

Growing Together
