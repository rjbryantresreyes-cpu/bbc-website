What it is

Stock Footage, Photos & Music, in plain words

These are the libraries we draw from to fill out your videos with background footage, images, and sound. Everything we use is free to use and cleared for it, so your content looks polished without a stack of expensive stock licenses landing on your bill. Good background footage and the right music are what make a simple message feel like a real production instead of a phone clip.

How we use it

How Balay ni Bruno & Co. puts it to work

When we build or edit a video, we pull matching background footage, photos, and music from these free-to-use libraries and drop them in legally. It fills the gaps between your main points and gives every clip a finished, produced feel.

Common questions

What business owners ask us about this

Is the footage and music really free to use?

Yes. We source from libraries that offer media cleared for commercial use, so you are not exposed to licensing trouble.

Will my videos look like everyone else's?

We choose media to match your specific message and blend it with your branding, so the video still feels like yours, not a generic template.

Can you use my own footage or photos too?

Yes. If you have your own images or clips, we will use those and fill the gaps with cleared stock where needed.

Could I get a copyright strike from the music?

The music we use is cleared for this, which is the whole point of sourcing it this way rather than grabbing a popular song.
