How AI Finds Customers and Follows Up For You

Hunting for new customers by hand, one name at a time, is not a skill problem. It's a time problem. Here are the six real workflows we run, from the first lead found to the invoice that gets you paid.

Finding customers and staying on top of them afterward is not one job. It's six, and this video walks through the real workflow behind each one.

Each chapter above is also its own full write-up, linked so you can go deeper on whichever piece matters most to your business right now.

None of these six workflows work in isolation. A lead gets found, a follow-up keeps the conversation alive, a call gets summarized automatically, an intake gathers everything up front, and a proposal and an invoice both look like they came from the same trusted business. That's the whole loop, from first lead to paid invoice.

Key Takeaways

  • An AI can hunt and qualify leads, but the first real conversation stays yours.
  • Most leads go cold from a missed follow-up, not a no. A system that tracks timing fixes that.
  • A meeting recorder plus an AI summary turns a call into a drafted follow-up in under two minutes.
  • One structured intake replaces weeks of scattered back and forth with new clients.
  • Proposals and invoices are brand touchpoints too, and both can be automated while a human still approves and sends.
