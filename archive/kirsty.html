About Kirsty

A Versatile Creative Who Keeps Things Moving

Kirsty brings a rare combination to every project she joins, creative depth across content and design, paired with the operational discipline to keep things organized and on schedule. She doesn't just make things look good. She builds systems that make the work sustainable.

Her expertise spans social media management, graphic design, video editing, email marketing, SEO writing, and AI-assisted workflow support. She works at the intersection of content and operations, producing assets, managing platforms, and keeping the backend organized so nothing falls through the cracks.

At BBC, Kirsty currently supports audience engagement operations for All Ryze — contributing to outreach, communication workflows, and multi-platform engagement under Ryan Bernaldez's operations management. Her broader skill set makes her a flexible asset across BBC's full creative and operational support spectrum.

Working Approach

How Kirsty Works

The principles and frameworks that guide Kirsty's approach across every project she supports.

01

Content-First Thinking

Every graphic, video, and post starts with the message, what needs to be said, who it's for, and which format serves it best. Design follows content, not the other way around.

02

Platform-Aware Execution

Different platforms need different content. Kirsty adapts format, tone, and scheduling to the native behavior of each platform, so content lands right wherever it's posted.

03

Visual Brand Consistency

Every asset created reinforces the brand it represents. Consistent colors, fonts, tone, and visual standards ensure that no piece of content ever looks out of place.

04

AI-Assisted Production

Where AI tools can accelerate quality output without sacrificing authenticity, Kirsty integrates them, using AI to generate, refine, and speed up creative and written work.

05

Relationship-Centered Engagement

Audience communication isn't mechanical. Real engagement means personalized, thoughtful interactions that build trust and keep relationships warm over time, at scale.

06

Operations-First Mindset

Creative work without organization creates chaos. Kirsty treats workflow discipline as part of the job, documenting, following systems, and keeping the backend accountable so nothing slips.
