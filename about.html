From the Founder

How a Family Decision
Became a Business Built on People

Before BBC existed, I was working in the call center industry here in the Philippines. When the pandemic hit, I made one decision — I wanted to work remotely so I could be home with my family.

I became a VA. I worked hard, kept learning, and kept building my skills. And the people closest to me — friends, cousins, relatives, colleagues — saw what I was doing and wanted to be part of it.

So I trained them myself. When client work grew, I brought in the people I had already invested in. No cold hiring. No random matching. Just people I trusted, working together the way family does.

That same spirit is how we work with every client today. Welcome to Balay ni Bruno — the house we built together.

RJ

RJ Bryan B. Tres Reyes

Founder, Balay ni Bruno & Co.

How It Started

It Started at Home —
and That's Still How It Runs

BBC didn't start with a business plan. It started with a decision to go remote during the pandemic, be present for family, and build something meaningful from that choice. RJ trained the people around him — friends, family members, colleagues — and together they became the foundation of what BBC is today.

Balay ni Bruno means the house of Bruno. That name reflects something real. This is not a platform or a faceless agency. It is a home — built by people who trust each other, run with care, and extended to every client who becomes part of it.

Your Journey With BBC

What It Looks Like to
Work With BBC

From your first call to long-term operational support — here is the full client journey.

Stage 01

Discovery Call

We start with a real conversation. The founder learns about your business, your current workload, your goals, and the kind of support you actually need. No forms. No pressure. Just clarity.

Stage 02

Onboarding & Setup

Once we're aligned, we move into onboarding. This includes a detailed questionnaire, account and tool access, workflow planning, and organizing everything your assistant needs to hit the ground running. The founder guides this entire stage personally.

Stage 03

Building Your Digital Foundation

This is where the real work begins. Depending on your needs, we build or organize your website, set up your digital marketing systems, create your content workflows, establish your operations structure, and get everything running from scratch or reorganized properly.

Stage 04

Planning & Campaigns

With the foundation in place, we move into active planning. We map out upcoming campaigns, content calendars, marketing priorities, and the next operational roadmap — so your business always has a clear direction and a team executing toward it.

Stage 05

Ongoing Support & Growth

Your assistant handles the ongoing workload while the founder stays involved for strategy, check-ins, and quarterly planning. We monitor performance, review analytics, identify what needs to improve, and keep building — so your business never stops moving forward.

Most BBC clients stay for years. Some pause and come back. Almost all of our new clients come from referrals. That's the kind of support we aim to build.

What We Stand For

The Values Behind
Every Client Relationship

These aren't posted on a wall. They're how we actually work.

01

People Before Process

We believe the most important thing in any client relationship is the human connection. Systems support people — they don't replace them. Every workflow we build exists to make someone's work life better.

02

Stay Involved

We don't match and disappear. The founder stays actively involved from discovery through setup, execution, and long-term strategy. Our clients are never left to figure things out alone.

03

Organized by Default

Clarity and structure are not extras at BBC — they are the baseline. Every engagement starts with a plan, every system is documented, and every workflow is built to be understood, not just completed.

04

Trusted Collaboration

BBC is built on trust — between the founder, the team, and the client. Every assistant is chosen carefully. Every relationship is treated like a long-term partnership, not a transaction.

05

Build for the Long Term

We don't optimize for quick wins. We build systems, workflows, and operational structures that grow with the business — and we stay involved to make sure they continue to improve.

06

Family-Rooted

The name Balay ni Bruno — the house of Bruno — reflects something real. This agency is built like a home: with care, with people who trust each other, and with a genuine commitment to doing things right.
