About Dexter

A Senior Creative Who Covers Design and Video End to End

Dexter is a senior graphic designer and video editor. He works across the full creative range, from brand and advertising design to reels, short-form clips, and long-form video, and brings a polished, production-ready eye to everything he touches.

On the design side he builds brand graphics, advertising concepts, and marketing visuals. On the video side he edits reels and social content as comfortably as he handles longer, more involved pieces. Design and motion, in one person.

At Balay ni Bruno & Co., Dexter joins the creative team supporting S. Riviere Hair Collection, bringing senior-level craft to the brand's content and campaigns.
