How We Get Any Local Business on Google's First Page

Somewhere near a business, a customer just searched for exactly what it does, and found someone else instead. Not because they're better. Because Google never learned the business exists. Here are the six real workflows we run to fix that, without a bigger ad budget.

Getting found on Google is not one trick. It is six things working together, and this video walks through all six, the same way we run them for our own partnerships.

None of these six workflows work in isolation. A complete Google Business Profile feeds every other page. Every finished job becomes a page. Every city you serve gets its own page. And the one number, cost per booked job, is what proves the whole thing is actually working.

Key Takeaways

  • Getting found on Google starts free, with a complete Google Business Profile and genuine reviews.
  • Every finished job is proof of work Google can show to the next customer, if it becomes its own page.
  • A business that serves several towns needs a dedicated page for each one, not one generic page.
  • Urgent searches, like storm damage, convert higher than any other kind of local search.
  • You cannot know if marketing is working until you can measure cost per booked job, not clicks.
