About John

The Person Who Keeps the Content Coming

John is a content creator and marketing support specialist at Balay ni Bruno & Co. He runs the day-to-day content engine, posting to social media, designing the graphics that go with each post, and producing video from short reels to longer pieces.

His work spans both BBC's own channels and the brands BBC supports. Whatever the platform, John keeps the feed active, the visuals sharp, and the message consistent, so a brand shows up every day instead of going quiet.

Platform-savvy and dependable, John is the steady hand behind a consistent presence, turning ideas into finished posts, graphics, and video that are ready to publish.
