6. Two things built in on purpose

Every text can be stopped. Each one gives people a way out. That is not optional, it is what keeps your phone number from being shut down by the carriers. It costs you almost nothing, because someone who opts out was never going to buy.
Nothing promises a result. No message says we guarantee anything about someone's credit. Every email carries the line about results being individual and people having the right to dispute things themselves for free. In your industry that line is what keeps you clean.
