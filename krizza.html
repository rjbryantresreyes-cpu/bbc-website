About Krizza

Consistent, Organized, and Genuinely Good at Keeping Things Moving

Krizza manages the full social media and operational layer for the clients she supports — content creation, scheduling, CRM management, and client-facing communications all handled in one place. She's the person who keeps brands active and relationships healthy without it feeling like effort.

She works at the intersection of content and operations. On the content side: social media management, content calendars, creative production. On the operations side: CRM systems through HubSpot, client management, builder outreach, and keeping the backend organized so everything runs smoothly.

At BBC, Krizza serves as BBC Manager for Wooden Woodworks — managing their social presence, content strategy, CRM, and operational support. When things need to stay on track and on brand, Krizza makes sure they do.

Working Approach

How Krizza Works

01

Consistency Over Volume

A brand that posts consistently always beats one that posts a lot and then disappears. Krizza builds sustainable content rhythms — not sprint-and-crash cycles.

02

Brand Voice Before Anything

Every caption, post, and message sounds like the brand it represents. Krizza establishes and maintains voice guidelines so nothing ever feels off-brand or inconsistent.

03

CRM as a Relationship Tool

HubSpot isn't just a database — it's a system for keeping relationships warm. Krizza uses CRM data to time follow-ups, track conversations, and never let a prospect go cold.

04

Content That Serves Sales

Social content is connected to business goals. Every post is considered in terms of what it does for the brand's audience relationships and ultimately for pipeline generation.

05

Operations as a Competitive Advantage

When the backend runs well, everything else flows. Krizza keeps the operational layer clean — so the client and the team can focus on growth instead of firefighting.

06

Outreach Built on Research

Builder outreach isn't cold messaging — it's targeted, researched, and relationship-oriented. Krizza identifies the right people before reaching out and leads with genuine value.
